# Built-in trips: what to confirm before selling

The built-in trips (catalog version 2) use real hotels, restaurants and attractions, with the
prices they published or listed in 2026. Several official sites couldn't be opened while the
catalog was researched, so some prices are estimates. Before selling a date, call or book each
place and, if a price changed, update the trip in **Admin → Trips**: change the price line, then
use **Costs & profit → Use $…** to get the new selling price.

Things that apply to every trip:

- **The van** — real daily rate with insurance, whether the rental contract allows paying
  passengers and (for the U.S.) crossing the border, and its height: many underground garages
  take only 1.65–2.1 m, so a 12-passenger van often needs a surface lot.
- **Groups of 8–12** — most restaurants want a reservation (some only take group menus from 20
  people, so the group orders from the regular menu), and hotels give room blocks from about
  5 rooms through their group sales office.
- **Prices with tax and tip** — meal costs in the price lines include 13% HST and an 18% tip.
  U.S. prices were converted at 1.38.

## Day trips

### Niagara Falls classic day (`niagara-falls-dia`)
- Niagara City Cruises child fare (two sources: $32.95 and $27.95 + HST) and Journey Behind
  the Falls ($28 or $33 + HST depending on the date).
- Lunch at Table Rock House (~$58) and dinner at Rainforest Cafe (~$46) are estimates; Table Rock
  House group menus start at 20 people. Rainforest Cafe group bookings: Falls Avenue Resort sales.
- Niagara Parks parking for the van (assumed $35).

### Niagara-on-the-Lake wine route (`niagara-on-the-lake-vino`)
- Not verified online: Inniskillin tasting (~$28), Trius sparkling cellar tour (~$34) and lunch
  (~$85), Peller Estates 10Below (~$40), COWS on Queen Street. Confirm each winery takes a
  Saturday group of 8–12 with fixed times (Trius and Peller belong to the same company).

### Toronto in a day (`toronto-en-un-dia`)
- CN Tower and Ripley's Aquarium prices (from listings, not the official pages).
- Mill St. Brew Pub dinner (~$39) and Carousel Bakery lunch (~$21) are estimates.
- Parking for a 12-passenger van downtown (surface lots).

### Canada's Wonderland (`canadas-wonderland`)
- Ticket price on the date (online "as low as $45" + HST + up to $9.99 fee), whether children
  pay less, group-ticket minimum, the 2027 season calendar, parking for the van.
- New rule: guests 15 and under need a companion aged 21 or older.
- Lazy Bear Lodge meal (~$33) and the All-Day Dining add-on price.

### Pelee Island (`pelee-island`)
- Saturday sailing times for the date (Leamington dock until late July, then Kingsville), fares
  per leg, and the vehicle fare for the van's height (high-roof vans pay $33 per leg). Van spaces
  on summer weekends sell out months ahead.
- Winery Pavilion group tour time (12:00) and season; Heritage Centre admission.

### Point Pelee & Essex wineries (`point-pelee-vinos-essex`)
- Park admission for a tour group (individual $10 vs commercial group rate), the Tip shuttle
  season, Oxley lunch prices and group booking (8–18 people), CREW tasting price and hours.

### Frankenmuth & Birch Run outlets (`frankenmuth-outlets`)
- Not verified online: Zehnder's chicken dinner (~US$33), Tony's I-75 Restaurant (open, Saturday
  hours, seats for the group), outlet and Bronner's hours, Ambassador Bridge toll for the van.

### Detroit: Motown and Mexicantown (`detroit-motown`)
- Not verified online: Motown Museum prices and timed tours, DIA non-resident prices, Supino
  seating for the group, Xochimilco open in 2027 (alternatives: El Barzón, Taqueria El Rey),
  tunnel/bridge tolls and parking.

### Grand Bend beach (`grand-bend-playa`)
- Industria Pizzeria + Bar (Grand Bend) open and taking groups; Chuck's Roadhouse address
  (41 or 14 Main St W) and group bookings; Pinery permit for a tour van; beach parking.
- Smackwater Jack's (the old dinner stop) has closed.

### Toronto Christmas market (`mercado-navideno-toronto`)
- Distillery Winter Village dates and ticket rules (tickets from 4 pm Friday–Sunday; children
  9 and under free), parking near the Distillery (limited by construction), SOMA Chocolatemaker
  open, and whether the Nathan Phillips Square rink is back after the 2026 World Cup.

## Weekends and tours

### Niagara Fallsview weekend (`niagara-fallsview-fin-de-semana`)
- **Embassy Suites by Hilton Niagara Falls Fallsview** — priced at an average of $600/night
  all-in for a 2-queen suite (summer Saturdays ~$725, May/June/Sept/Oct ~$450–600); check-in and
  check-out times, the daily mandatory charge, and whether the van fits the garage ($70/day).
- Skylon early dinner (4:30/5:00 pm seatings, ~$88 with fees, tax and the automatic 18% tip for
  8+), Queen Victoria Place lunch season, Power Station hours, Clifton Hill Fun Pass price.

### Toronto weekend (`toronto-fin-de-semana`)
- **Chelsea Hotel** — taking bookings for your dates, the rate (~$360 all-in), rooms with
  **2 double beds**, the Bed & Breakfast rate (~$30 pp), and a lot for the van nearby: its
  garage only takes 1.65 m (~$35/night assumed).
- Casa Morales (Kensington Market) group booking — it replaces Seven Lives, which closed in
  September 2026. El Catrín dinner (~$52). Casa Loma, Toronto Island ferry fares.

### Muskoka fall colours (`muskoka-colores-otono`)
- **Deerhurst Resort** fall rate (~$387/night all-in with the $39.95 resort fee, 4% MAT and HST)
  and a 4–5 room block; the Saturday dinner buffet on your dates.
- Algonquin: buy the vehicle permit up to 5 days ahead and confirm a 12-passenger van pays the
  regular permit ($21), not the $125 bus permit. Dorset Lookout Tower commercial rate and fall
  booking; Arrowhead permit; The Portage Store's October hours.

### Blue Mountain ski & spa (`blue-mountain-esqui-spa`)
- Not verified online: **The Westin Trillium House** winter rate (~$460/night all-in), parking,
  Oliver & Bonacini Café Grill (dinner and breakfast), the Saturday beginner package
  (~$185: lesson, rentals, beginner lifts) and Scandinave Spa (~$120, 19+ only).

### Tobermory & the Bruce Peninsula (`tobermory-bruce`)
- Not verified online: **Blue Bay Motel** (32 Bay St S; rooms, summer rate ~$360 all-in),
  Cyprus Lake (Grotto) parking reservation in 4-hour slots and whether a tour van needs a
  Parks Canada business permit, park admission, the glass-bottom boat with Flowerpot Island
  drop-off (~$70 adult / $48 child: Blue Heron Co. or Bruce Anchor Cruises), Craigie's breakfast
  hours (by 7 am), Tobermory Brewing Co. & Grill and The Fish & Chip Place for a group.

### Ottawa & Montréal (`ottawa-montreal`)
- Parliament tour times (free; book at rts.parl.ca — Centre Block is closed, tours visit the
  Senate and the West Block).
- **Lord Elgin Hotel** (~$320) and **Hôtel Bonaventure** (~$380) rates for your dates; avoid the
  Grand Prix and Jazz Festival weekends in Montréal. Van parking: the Lord Elgin valet takes only
  1.87 m (use the City Hall garage, 7'1"); tall vans pay $59.99/day at the Bonaventure.
- Schwartz's and La Banquise take no reservations: a group may wait or be split.

### Québec City & Montréal (`quebec-montreal`)
- The tour now sleeps the last night in Montréal (2 nights in Québec, 2 in Montréal), so no day
  has more than ~880 km of driving for one driver-guide.
- Montmorency cable-car and parking prices (Sépaq), **Le Concorde** rating (3★) and check-in time,
  outdoor parking for the van (its garage takes 1.83 m), summer weekend rates.
- Pub du Mitan group bookings (by email, June 1–October 10), Le Lapin Sauté hours, Ashton prices,
  and traffic on the Île d'Orléans bridge (being replaced).

### Chicago (`chicago`)
- **Palmer House** rate (~$395 CAD all-in), the daily destination charge and whether the van fits
  the self-park garage; Gordie Howe bridge toll (C$8 per crossing under 2.3 m, more for taller
  vans).
- Architecture cruise and Skydeck prices on your dates (they change with demand; children pay
  full price on the cruise), Centennial Wheel price, Navy Pier parking.
- Carnitas Uruapan (Little Village, dine-in) group booking; Portillo's and Lou Malnati's prices.
- U.S. entry: travellers who aren't Canadian citizens need a U.S. visa (Mexican citizens can't use
  ESTA) and a valid document to re-enter Canada.
