Express — Add products (private browser extension)
====================================================

What it does
- On walmart.ca and amazon.ca product pages, an "Add to Express" button appears
  at the bottom left. Press it and the product (name, photos, price, brand,
  category, description, stock) goes to your Express admin:
  __ADMIN__/admin/productos/agregar
- There you check the list, fix anything, and press "Add to the store".
  Prices follow your rules in Settings → Prices.
- Shortcut: Alt+Shift+E (Option+Shift+E on a Mac) does the same as the
  button. Change it in chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
- On other stores, open a product and press the Express button in the
  browser's toolbar (the puzzle piece → pin "Express — Add products").
- It only reads the page you're looking at, when you press the button.

Install (Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer)
1. Unzip this file (double-click it). You get a folder "express-add-products".
2. Open chrome://extensions (in Edge: edge://extensions).
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
4. Press "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder (the one that has
   manifest.json inside).
5. Keep that folder: don't delete or move it, the browser uses it.

Tips
- On walmart.ca, choose your Walmart store first (top of the page): prices and
  stock follow the store you choose.
- Sign in to your Express admin in the same browser.
- New version: download the extension again from the admin, replace the
  folder's files, then press the reload arrow on the extension in
  chrome://extensions.
